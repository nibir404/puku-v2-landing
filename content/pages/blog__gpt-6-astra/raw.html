GPT-6 Astra is now available in Devin

Cognition|September 3, 2026|1 min read|

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra. It is available now in Devin Desktop and Devin CLI, and is also part of the model mixture in Devin Cloud.

On FrontierCode 1.1, our proprietary benchmark that grades models on real-world engineering tasks based on quality and mergeability, GPT-6 Astra surpasses Fable 5.1. It is second only to Fable 5 (within 0.4 points) and 64% cheaper.

When powering Devin’s testing capabilities, GPT-6 Astra achieves SOTA results on our internal testing benchmark. It drives tests that are more comprehensive, with clearer reports and more user-friendly video evidence.
