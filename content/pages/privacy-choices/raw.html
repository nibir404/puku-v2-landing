Your privacy choices

Manage how your data is used for advertising on Devin.

Opt-out preference

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising purposes under applicable privacy laws, including the CCPA/CPRA and similar US state laws.

Do not sell or share my personal information

When enabled, we will honor your opt-out preference and stop selling or sharing your personal information for targeted advertising purposes, as required by applicable law in your jurisdiction.

Status:Not opted out

What changes when you opt out

Blocked

  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel
  • Reddit Pixel
  • X (Twitter) conversion tracking
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag
  • Bing / Microsoft UET
  • Google Ads conversion tracking
  • Google Analytics (analytics only)
  • Conversions API (server-side: Meta, Google, Reddit, X, LinkedIn)
  • Other third-party advertising tags

About Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we automatically treat you as opted out. Where required by applicable law, this opt-out cannot be reversed for 12 months from the date the signal was first detected, even if you later disable GPC in your browser.
